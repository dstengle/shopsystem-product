# Request: how the lead shop builds its own scenarios

## 1. What is requested

The product authority, 2026-09-09, on the finding that a build run by
the lead shop on its own assignment does not roll up into an
initiative's cost because no process defines it: "Park this for a
moment. The discussion of doing builds in the lead shop needs some
brainstorming work. Please set up a new request for this."

What stands: scenario-assignment's dispatch step sends scenarios to a
Bounded Context and reconcile-and-close verifies that shop's
response; nothing defines the step where the lead shop builds an
assignment it holds itself. Gap lead-ki66p records this since the
first internal assignment. Every internal build so far (feat-role-
decisions through feat-plain-voice-sections) ran as an architect
launched directly on a bead no process opened; the cost-row tool
refuses such a bead ("the start comment names no *-process id").

## 2. From whom

Reader: the lead-pm role. Originator: the product authority. Received
through the lead shop's operational contract (lead-4kymc), in
conversation.

## 3. Route

Route said by the lead-pm role, 2026-09-09: **discovery**, form
brainstorm. Why: the ask is for thinking before a definition, and the
question reaches the shape of the flow — whether the lead shop's own
build is a branch of scenario-assignment, a process of its own, or a
Bounded Context's work — which a bet must settle. Parked: the
discovery opens when the authority opens it, not before.

Originator's answer: **accepted** — the route is the authority's own
ask.

## 4. Result

Routed to the initiative
[init-implementation-process](../initiatives/init-implementation-process.md),
framed and bet in the discovery conversation of 2026-09-09 (anchor
lead-z2nit, session record sess-2026-09-09-c). The brainstorm took the
question past the lead shop: the system defines no implementation
process and no implementation roles, and the lead shop's undefined
builds exposed it. Two things were split off and are not in that bet:
the amendment to the `delivery-verified` principle, which needs a
request of its own and is not yet recorded; and the Bounded Context
designation — what a context is responsible for, who depends on it,
and how that dependency is met — carried to the operational-contract
discovery (lead-bmmzh).
