# Request: simplify the product process and the language

## 1. What is requested

The product authority, 2026-09-08:

"We need a much more drastic approach. We need to remove checks and
cold reads, much of the feedback is verbose nitpicking. The language
needs to be drastically simplified and much more terse. Otherwise we
will never get anything done. Quality checks will need to move into a
review sweep mechanism. High quality prompts and improvement
mechanisms are good, huge numbers of check cycles are not."

"The product process itself and the role split requiring human
interaction has become a big problem. We need to remove all of the
human checks throughout the process."

"What is the point of the briefs? In a lot of cases they seem to be
posing decision questions for work already done and as far as I can
tell there is no state in the system actually requiring those
decisions."

"ADR writing should be AFTER the features are written."

"Feature creation prompt needs significant tightening."

"Checks - not on session close. Repairs run as new requests."
"Human steps - complete after discovery and framing."
"Language - agree but will need testing and verification."
"Briefs - drop." "lead-pm is appropriate for discovery."
"Session reviews on request only."
"Existing definitions to new language. Foundational artifacts are
creating bloat."

Evidence read in the conversation: 313 cold screens since
2026-08-23; the themes unglossed terms (87), stale counts (77),
wobbly (76), length (64); six fed back into a definition.
