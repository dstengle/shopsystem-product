## Context

PDR-032 owns the artifact type system as one bundled taxonomy. The per-type
contracts — what each of the eight kinds (adr, pdr, brief, intent-record,
candidate, session-record, current-state, prioritization-record) is for and
distinctly requires — are legible to the generator but not individually stated as
authoritative docs. pdr-035 established the kinds and the two-views requirement;
adr-067 established the base schema. This slice states the *needs* per kind so
each kind's contract stands on its own and PDR-032's taxonomy can be superseded
without loss.

## Options considered

- **Leave the taxonomy bundled in PDR-032.** Rejected: no per-type authoritative
  needs, and PDR-032 cannot be cleanly superseded by the new family — a founding
  doc stays in the accepted set carrying content the new docs also claim.
- **Eight separate per-type needs PDRs.** Rejected for now: eight near-parallel
  docs are heavier to author and review while proposed; the per-type content is
  better seen together first. (Re-split remains open for the authority.)
- **One sectioned per-type needs PDR** covering all eight kinds (this decision),
  paired with one per-type schema ADR.

## Decision

For each of the eight kinds, state its **needs** — why it exists, its role in the
workflow, its characteristic sections, and its participation on the provenance
spine and in the base schema's edges — as concepts, not field syntax. Authored as
**one sectioned PDR** (one section per kind); the matching per-type **schema** is
the paired ADR, additive on the base schema (slice #2).

The needs statement pairs with, and does not duplicate, the schema: this PDR says
*what each kind is for and needs*; the schema ADR says *what fields/sections each
kind adds on the base*. On acceptance this PDR (with slices #1 and #2) **jointly
supersedes PDR-032** — the N:M supersession — re-homing PDR-032's founding,
base-field, and per-type-taxonomy content across #1/#2/#3 respectively.

**Granularity note (flagged for ratification):** one-doc-per-lane rather than
per-type docs is the router's autonomous call under the draft's open-slicing
latitude; the authority may re-split at acceptance.

## Consequences

- Each kind gets an authoritative needs statement; the type system is legible per
  kind rather than as one bundled taxonomy.
- PDR-032 can be jointly superseded with no orphaned content — subject to a
  coverage check (every PDR-032 taxonomy claim lands in some per-type section)
  that the paired schema ADR's dispatch must include.
- The paired schema ADR (built on adr-067) carries the additive per-type schemas
  and the **current-state versioned-vs-singleton resolution**; that decision is
  made there, not here.
- On acceptance, the joint supersession of PDR-032 across #1/#2/#3 is materialized
  and `current-state.md incorporates` updated for all three.
