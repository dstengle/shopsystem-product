## Context

The knowledge system validates artifacts (per-file `validate` + the coherence
gate) but nothing guides their creation. The result is repeated lifecycle
jumping: freehand decision docs with no provenance, wrong starting `status`, the
withdrawn ADR-067/PDR-035-draft, and a lead-pm ADR misstep on 2026-07-25 caught
only by the product authority. Slice #3 gives each kind a per-type schema; this
slice makes each kind's authoring *guided* by a writing skill and guarantees every
kind has one.

## Options considered

- **Rely on the gate alone.** Rejected: the gate is after-the-fact; authors still
  improvise and the authority still catches jumps by hand.
- **Documentation/convention only** (a written "how to author" guide). Rejected:
  unenforced convention is what we have; it drifts and is skipped under pressure.
- **Per-type writing skills + blocking enforcement** (this decision): each kind
  has a skill bundling its template and schema checks, and shop-templates enforces
  every kind has a valid one.

## Decision

Every one of the eight kinds gets a **writing skill** that bundles the kind's
template (`shop-knowledge template <kind>`) and schema checks
(`shop-knowledge validate`) and walks the author through the kind's lifecycle —
provenance edges to set, correct starting `status`, required sections. Skills
**reuse the single-sourced template/schema**, never copy them. **shop-templates
enforces**, blocking, that every recognized kind has a writing skill and that each
skill's content is valid (points at the live template/schema, covers the required
sections).

Lane homes (per the draft): this needs PDR; the skill-template structure and the
enforcement mechanism as ADRs; role behaviour + enforcement realized in
shopsystem-templates; gate rules in scenarios. **Mechanism calls deferred to the
ADRs:** one-skill-parameterized-by-kind vs eight discrete skills, and whether the
writing skill also injects relevant accepted-decision context at authoring time
(the consult-decision-index coupling, lead-x7bp lineage).

## Consequences

- Authoring is guided at creation time; the authority stops catching lifecycle
  jumps by hand. This is the counterpart to the coherence gate and the second
  half of "the document and role system is not up and running fully."
- Enforcement being blocking means a kind cannot ship without a valid writing
  skill — closing the gap rather than papering it.
- On acceptance, the skill-template structure and enforcement dispatch to
  **shopsystem-templates** (role/skill discipline) with gate rules to scenarios;
  the writing skills reuse the knowledge BC's template/schema surface, so no
  second copy is introduced.
- Depends on slice #3's per-type schemas as the check target; the ADRs are
  authored after adr-069.
