## Outcome

**Mode: discovery** (lead-pm as interlocutor; work item `lead-jacwu`,
brainstorm form). **The authority converged**; the frame step recorded
[`init-skills-availability`](../initiatives/init-skills-availability.md)
`proposed`. The initiative: approved process definitions are not
available to the executing agent at its point of work, and making a
skill available belongs to no process; the outcome is that an agent
operates from the approved definition of its activity, delivered as a
skill through a process with its own check.

### Positions reached in dialogue (authority's words quoted)

- **Scope ruling.** "This is very constrained and limited to making
  sure skills are available to the agent. This should itself be a
  process." And on rendering: "Rendering is a tool run and should be a
  process, which is also missing."
- **Channel ruling.** "Almost everything at this point should be
  skills-related other than parts of role definitions."
- **Guidelines.** "Guidlines are part of the artifact process and this
  is stated in the glossary but it looks unimplemented in practice."
  Filed as `lead-m2o7h`; a no-go for this initiative.
- **Import.** "Import stays out" — brief-030's 38-skill plan is not in
  scope; migration stays demand-pull.
- **The stakes.** "This session is showing that we are already
  drifting away from the principles and need to start implementing
  consistency in the process and tools." The observed drift instances:
  the compiler run is an orphan activity (`no-orphan-activities`); the
  loadable skills location is a hand-channel (`governed-context`); the
  one loadable skill diverges from its generated source
  (`single-source-of-truth`).
- **Backlog direction, verbatim:** "Add to backlog for next: ADR
  artifact and processes." Recorded as `lead-mfcyp`.
