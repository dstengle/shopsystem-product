## Outcome

**Mode: discovery** (lead-pm). Closed at the authority's clear-prep
instruction, on a **mode correction that governs the resume**: "I feel like
we're in the wrong mode here. I want to have a dialog about the statements
I'm making, not just steering the current activity." The router had been
converting the authority's reflective statements into recorded directives and
launched evidence-gathering; the authority wants the statements *engaged* — a
thinking dialogue — before anything is operationalized. The next session
resumes as that dialogue, with the anchors below as its agenda; nothing new
launches until it has happened.

Landed this session: pdr-039 amended per the authority's review (scope
honesty, R8 demoted, definitions named prerequisite; acceptance held); the
rebaseline bill sitting deferred; the set-level question opened; the
artifact-definition packet produced and persisted to
`drafts/artifact-definition-packet.md` (sitting material, NOT ratified — read
it with its §-final verification annex: 14 confirmed / 9 weakened / 5 refuted
/ 2 omissions; the annex wins over the body). The common-practice atlas was
deliberately NOT launched — stopped by the authority's interrupt, parked
pending the dialogue.

Authority verdicts on pdr-039 (anchors, verbatim in substance):

- "It is taking the form of a principle set, but it is by no means an
  exhaustive, well-thought-out ruleset for what should go into decision
  documents."
- "It uses the generic term artifacts, but only ADRs were reviewed, when PDRs
  are currently the main decision driver in the system — I'm not sure that
  ADRs and PDRs can be governed by the same ruleset. I actually have a good
  idea of what an ADR should have. I've been trusting the system with respect
  to PDRs as a format and what a good one looks like."
- "R8 … depends on one observation quoted from me and could be dangerous if
  embraced as a rule. A 'plane' can contain many things."
- "Key artifacts like a brief are not just decision records."
- "We are trying to repair the corpus without defining how a good artifact
  (decision or otherwise) is constructed in the first place … the ruleset in
  pdr-039 may turn out to have very large gaps."
- "I see no reason to proceed without a strong definition for these artifacts
  that makes it simple and clear when they are good enough."

Second directive, same session (the set-level deepening):

- "Do not assume that these are the right artifacts. I am not at all sure that
  the current set and workflow are what we need and are easily resolved
  against common practices. There is a huge body of work in the world on the
  many ways to do product management. I've heard of a brief, but never heard
  of a PDR. One professional product manager was confused when I said PDR and
  not PRD."
- "The system has accomplished many things and there is a core of the
  artifacts and many written and implemented scenarios that represent an
  actual working system, so this hasn't at all been a waste. But it is
  pointing towards a limit on scalability of the concepts. I thought that
  could be fixed with progressive disclosure, but that has led deeper and
  deeper into a rabbit hole that questions the foundations of the system."

Actions taken at open: pdr-039 amended (scope honesty; R8 demoted to bounded
observation; prerequisite named) — acceptance stays held. Rebaseline bill
sitting deferred; the bill (drafts/rebaseline-bill.md) stands as census.
Confirming evidence recorded: pdr-037's per-kind needs half was never written;
adr/pdr schemas differ only by decision-makers plus one section name.

## Open threads

- **The set-level question now precedes per-kind definitions**: whether the
  current eight kinds and their workflow are the right artifacts at all,
  resolved against common product-management practice. Vocabulary must anchor
  to terms the world knows (the PDR/PRD confusion is the type specimen);
  bespoke concepts survive only where the system's genuinely novel needs
  (agent-operated, machine-checkable, provenance-enforced) justify them —
  "bespoke because needed" vs "bespoke by accident."
- Two evidence tracks in flight, independent: (1) the definition-foundation
  pack (per-kind job archaeology, verdict ledger, candidate definitions,
  definition format); (2) a common-practice atlas (decision-recording,
  product-definition/commitment, discovery, decomposition/traceability,
  agent-native documentation, state/status practice — surveyed with sources).
  When both land, a resolution map joins them: for each job this system needs
  done, what common practice calls the artifact that does it, and where the
  current set diverges with or without cause.
- Revised sitting sequence: sitting 0 decides the artifact SET from the
  resolution map; per-kind definitions follow for surviving kinds only.
- lead-jozud.2 remains the home: artifact contract set = set question + kind
  definitions + RACI, one body of work.
- Standing acknowledgment to preserve: the working core (dispatch machinery,
  implemented scenarios, the artifact spine that functions) is real and not a
  waste; what is hitting the scaling wall is the bespoke conceptual
  superstructure, and progressive disclosure was treating a symptom of that
  concept-complexity.
- Deferred behind all of this: the bill sitting, all rebaseline rewrites,
  pdr-039 acceptance.
