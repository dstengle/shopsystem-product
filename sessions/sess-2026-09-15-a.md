### What the conversation moved

The authority opened the resumption with a recollection of where the
held conversation had gone: "The last session we had I wanted to
remove the delivery-verified principle due to the implementation being
too complex for the system as it stands right now."

**The record did not hold that.** What stood in the corpus was the
09-09 pressure — "take care with delivery-verified. It could result in
a lot of bottlenecks and extra work if it is dogmatic and put in the
wrong place" — and the 09-10 opening statement quoted in the request's
§4, which pointed the other way: "if it can work I'd like to make it
happen and we can get away from making this split." Both are
amendment. Neither is removal. The lead-pm put that gap rather than
taking the recollection as the record.

**Why the record did not hold it.** The 2026-09-10 conversation ran
three `observe`/`engage` exchanges on lead-vfaj4 and never reached
`handoff`. Nothing landed: no session record, no initiative draft,
nothing committed but the route paragraph written into the request.
The anchor carries the step and report comments alone — the statements
are process-local data that only the session record carries, and no
session record was written. The authority's recollection was therefore
the only surviving evidence of where those exchanges went. This is the
`delivery-verified` conversation demonstrating the failure mode the
`session-handoff` process exists to prevent.

**The fork the lead-pm put (exchange 1).** Is the complexity in the
**bar** — that something must be shown to work at all — or in the
**mechanism**, "demonstrated in the running system"? The small-change
lane's verifying observation (one command from the repository root,
exit status 0, its output the evidence) was named as a demonstration
form already in force and costing nothing, as the cheap answer to the
mechanism reading.

**The authority closed the fork (exchange 2).** "There is no mechanism
that would be possible to implement in the current system that would
meet the principle. This will need to be brought back when the system
is more mature." Neither reading: no implementable mechanism exists
today at all, so the principle cannot be met rather than being
expensive to meet.

**Two open points put back, with recommendations (exchange 2).**
Whether the whole principle goes or only the mechanism statement —
the second statement is a prohibition needing no mechanism, but
keeping it alone would require renaming a principle whose name *is*
the mechanism; recommendation, remove whole. And how "bring it back"
survives a deletion that leaves the intent only in Document History —
recommendation, park a request naming the maturity conditions.

**The authority answered (exchange 3).** "I will bring it back in the
future" — read as declining the parked request: the reinstatement is
the authority's own to carry, not a mechanism the shop holds. The
remove-whole recommendation was not disputed; the lead-pm stated it
would proceed on that reading and invited correction. None came.

**Convergence (exchange 4).** The authority's word was "converge" —
the classification itself rather than the `observe` prompt's "bet".
Taken as convergence.

### The bet

Framing: `delivery-verified` stands in the working principle set and
no mechanism implementable in the system as it stands can meet it, so
every activity carries an obligation it cannot discharge — the
authority's words of 2026-09-15, on
req-2026-09-09-delivery-verified-amendment. For whom: every shop and
every session, the set being compiled into each one. Appetite and
no-gos: recorded in the initiative, framed after this record lands.

What the removal must sweep, as named in the conversation:
`basis/principles.md` (the principle and its fitness-screen column);
`basis/architecture-principles.md`, where `bidirectional-conformance`
cites it by name as the forward-direction flank of its rationale — the
paragraph reworded, that principle itself untouched;
[init-implementation-process](../initiatives/init-implementation-process.md),
whose no-go on changing the principle's text this supersedes; the
compiled `.claude/shop/principles.md`; and the request's own §4, whose
subject the conversation overtook — the ask was an amendment, the
answer is removal.
