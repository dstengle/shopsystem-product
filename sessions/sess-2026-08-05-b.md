## Outcome

**Mode: discovery** (lead-pm). Resumed as the dialogue directed by the
sess-2026-08-05-a mode correction; statements engaged as an interlocutor,
nothing operationalized until the authority directed the research step.

Converged positions (authority-confirmed in dialogue):

- **Construction precedes inspection.** "If we do not have a definition of
  good construction, we cannot have good checks." Checks that worked are
  outcome checks with reality as oracle (scenarios, hashes, schemas); craft
  has no oracle, so its checks must be projections of stated definitions —
  absent those, checks are retrofitted scar tissue and gameable.
- **Definitions are compiled, not shelved.** Definitions of good construction
  live in the generating context: the role prompt carries standing identity
  and cross-activity clarity; each discrete activity is captured in its own
  skill (token economy + diagnosability). Tools are wrapped in skills; not
  all skills wrap tools.
- **Attention architecture.** The authority ratifies the few, slow-changing
  definitions and spot-checks the many artifacts; mechanical checks cannot
  maintain themselves in an evolving system; a spot-check failure obligates a
  definition-or-check fix, not just an artifact fix. Spot-checking applies at
  every trust boundary (BC reviewer, lead reconciliation, authority).
- **Process principle (new).** Every activity in the system is part of a
  process; processes are clearly defined with expected outcomes, expected
  outputs, and possible resulting actions — including termination for
  long-running loops. Definition-level, not a workflow-system prescription.
  Root case: mechanism_observation died for lack of a consumer; 67
  unsanctioned bd memories grew in its place (load-bearing but ungoverned —
  triage into governed artifacts during re-founding).
- **Further principles confirmed or added:** gates-up-front (quantitative and
  qualitative); external-standards-first, bespoke only with justification;
  feedback loops with consumers built in (process, tools, prompts measured
  for effectiveness and updated); define-and-adhere-to-vocabulary (glossary +
  style guide first); governed generating context (no unsanctioned surfaces);
  delivery-verified-in-the-running-system (confirmed); single-source-of-truth
  /no-duplication (renamed from single-sourcing/no-mirrors, confirmed).
- **Sequencing.** Start with the product process — process, roles, and
  artifact types aligned with well-known product-management methods; process
  encoding governs lifecycle position, artifact schemas are defined
  structurally (independent of position/ownership), content-quality
  guidelines follow. BC code construction is deferred: it is already
  constrained by the tight scenario contract and will inherit the same
  compiled-prompt system.
- **Gherkin-fitness proposal (authority).** Given/When/Then scenarios as
  small qualitative fitness-test sets for non-deterministic generated output,
  explicitly not code-backed — recorded for evidence-based evaluation in the
  research, not yet adopted.
- **Seed meta-layer.** The definition regress terminates at a hand-ratified
  seed: the principles plus one definition-format meta-definition (statement,
  source, rationale, positive/negative examples, derived practices, derived
  checks, owner, ratification status). Before drafting it, the authority
  directed research into existing best practice to decide whether bespoke
  formats are needed at all.
