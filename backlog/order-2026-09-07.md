**Item 1 — init-tool-skills: the proof on the lint (feature-to-be).**

- **Framing served:** the planned initiative
  [init-tool-skills](../initiatives/init-tool-skills.md) (status:
  planned, v8; the authority's bet of 2026-09-07, taken in the

  lint, the tool every session runs — proven end to end. The decision

  answer as the skill's source. The product decision record for the
  go is made (pdr-2026-09-07-bet-tool-skills, in its check) and links
  here once checked.

  decomposition places in no Bounded Context has nothing to assign

  escalation applies.
