# Product decision record: the bet on init-skills-availability

## 1. Decision

The product authority takes the go on
[init-skills-availability](../initiatives/init-skills-availability.md),
betting the initiative's appetite — one working session of the lead
shop — on the capability-terms-repaired text of 2026-09-02: the
initiative is `planned` and its feature proceeds to feature authoring.

## 2. Alternatives

- **Hold — a fourth screen round on the repaired text.** Declined:
  the initiative-check reached its round cap at round 3 (judge
  claude-fable-5 / screen prompt v5), and round 3's single wobbly
  finding — scenario 4's scope over the broken-state description in
  the problem sentence — was settled by the authority itself, which
  directed the capability-terms repair of that sentence, applied
  post-cap. Another round would re-screen a sentence the authority
  had already ruled on.
- **No-go — decline the initiative.** Declined: the gap is live (one
  of sixteen approved processes reachable by the executing agent, and
  that one hand-diverged from its source) and the architect's
  feasibility verdict stands — the generating channel runs and its
  `source-digest` makes divergence mechanically checkable, within the
  appetite.

## 3. Consequences

- The initiative moves `proposed` → `planned`: for the lead shop,
  feature authoring on
  [feat-skills-availability](../features/feat-skills-availability.md)
  proceeds. Cost: the appetite — one working session of the lead
  shop — is committed to this initiative and is not available to
  other work.
- The bet is on the capability-terms-repaired text: for every later
  reader and check, that text is the text of record. Cost: round 3's
  wobbly finding is closed by the authority's direction rather than by
  a further screen round; the structural evidence stands in the
  initiative's §4 (For whom).
- The initiative's no-gos bind for the spent appetite: for the lead shop
  and any role that would take up that work, importing the
  frozen corpus's 38-skill plan, delivering guidelines into authoring
  contexts, role definitions beyond skill-shaped parts, and retrieval
  or knowledge-graph work are foreclosed for this initiative; each
  would need a new decision to reopen.

## 4. Reversibility

Reversible by a cancellation, carried by a later product decision
record under the same `bet` right and linking this one; before the
session is spent the cost of reversal is only that record, and after
it the session is sunk. Review trigger: the appetite is exhausted
without the outcome — an agent performing an activity loading the
approved definition of that activity through a governed channel with
its own check — demonstrated in the running system, or feature
authoring surfaces work outside the no-gos' boundary.
