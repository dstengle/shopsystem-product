    13	# Artifact type: initiative
    14	
    15	## Identity and ancestry
    16	
    17	- **Type:** `initiative` — the product-level problem artifact: one
    18	  problem worth solving, stated for the product, not for any Bounded
    19	  Context. It carries the outcome the problem serves and how that
    20	  outcome is measured, the appetite the product will spend, what it
    21	  will not do, and — once attached — the feasibility verdict and the
    22	  decomposition. It is the home of the framing: the PM role's recorded
    23	  statement of what a request is about is its first section and lives
    24	  nowhere else. Features are made from it and link it. It is one page
    25	  by rule, so its check is real and there is no second document to
    26	  keep in step. `ancestry` names no generic root: an initiative is
    27	  neither a request nor a definition, as the feature typedef also
    28	  states.
    29	- **Produced by:** the [PM role](../roles/lead-pm.md), in the
    30	  [discovery conversation](../processes/discovery-conversation.md) —
    31	  the role's assisting agent drafts, the PM decides its content.
    32	  Screened by the cold reviewer against the
    33	  [initiative fitness set](../fitness/initiative.fitness.md) in the
    34	  `initiative-check` process (pending — not yet authored; it mirrors
    35	  the PO output check with maker `lead-pm`, screener `cold-reviewer`,
    36	  and the authority deciding). That screen is the *check of record*:
    37	  the check whose verdict the definitions rely on, since the authority
    38	  holds the PM role in person and no other role checks the PM's
    39	  framing. What it checks is completeness and form — originator
    40	  quoted, one measure, a bound, no solution, attachments present. It
    41	  does not check whether the problem is worth solving; that value
    42	  judgment stands unchecked by design and rests on the recorded
    43	  originator's words and the measure. The authority *bets* on a
    44	  screened initiative — decides to spend the appetite — in a review.
    45	  The solutions architect role attaches the decomposition and its
    46	  feasibility verdict; the product designer role attaches its usability
    47	  evidence where the outcome has an interaction.
    48	  **Consumed by:** the PO role, which makes features from it; the
    49	  solutions architect role; the PO output check, where a feature's
    50	  `framing` input names this document's first section.
    51	
    52	## Required frontmatter
    53	
    54	`type: initiative`, `id`, `status`, `version`, `name`, `owner`
    55	(`lead-pm`), `created`, `updated`. `approved` does not apply; the
    56	lifecycle stands in. Status values and their writers:
    57	- `proposed` — the PM role, when the discovery conversation records it
    58	  (an amendment to that process, pending): the framed-but-unbet state.
    59	  A request declined here becomes `cancelled`, so the record survives.
    60	- `planned` — the authority's go/no-go, recorded as an outcome of the
    61	  [review conversation](../processes/review-conversation.md) (amendment
    62	  pending), replacing `proposed`.
    63	- `active` — the PO output check's record step, when the first feature
    64	  made from it passes (amendment pending: the step reads the feature's
    65	  `initiative` link).
    66	- `completed` — the [reconcile-and-close](../processes/reconcile-and-close.md)
    67	  process, when the last delivery under it is reconciled and the
    68	  measure is recorded against its target (amendment pending).
    69	- `cancelled` — the PM role's decision in a review, with the reason
    70	  recorded, from `proposed`, `planned`, or `active`.
    71	
    72	## Required sections
    73	
    74	1. **Framing** — the originator (who expressed the intent, quoted in
    75	   their own words), the problem taken to be worth solving, the outcome
    76	   it serves, and the contract — product or operational — it entered
    77	   through. The PM role's exclusive decision.
    78	2. **For whom** — who has the problem; one measure with its current
    79	   condition, quantified, and its target; and the interaction types the
    80	   outcome must hold on, from the
    81	   [core-task list](../experience/core-tasks.md), or "none" with the
    82	   reason.
    83	3. **Appetite** — the bound the product will spend — time or capacity
    84	   — that the features stay within; and the no-gos: what this
    85	   initiative will not do, each with its reason.
    86	4. **Feasibility and usability** — the solutions architect role's
    87	   feasibility verdict with its reasons, present; and, where §2 names
    88	   an interaction type, the product designer role's usability evidence
    89	   or the hypothesis it stands on, or "not yet" with the ask that
    90	   requests it.
    91	5. **Decomposition** — attached by the solutions architect role: the
    92	   Bounded Contexts the initiative touches, the relationship kind of
    93	   each contract between them it relies on, and the cross-context flow
    94	   in one place — the saga or process manager (the one component that
    95	   routes the flow between contexts) that will carry it, or "none" when
    96	   the contexts need no flow between them. "Not yet" until attached.
    97	6. **Features** — the features made from it, by id, as they are made;
    98	   empty until the first.
    99	
   100	## Rules
   101	
   102	- The framing is written here and nowhere else; a check that names the
   103	  framing as a criterion reads §1.
   104	- Sections 1–3 name no technology, structure, or interface form; an
   105	  interaction type named in §2 is a what.
   106	- At most 500 words outside the Document History; the bet must be
   107	  statable from §1 and §2 alone.
   108	- The go/no-go and a cancellation are each carried by a
   109	  [product decision record](product-decision-record.md) the PO role
   110	  makes for the decision and the PO output check screens, linked from
   111	  the Document History state entry.
   112	- The count of initiatives whose §5 names more than one Bounded
   113	  Context, per quarter, is a rendering the solutions architect role
   114	  reads through its interface with the PO role; a rising count is its
   115	  signal to review the decomposition, recorded as an architecture
   116	  decision record.
   117	
   118	## Commitment (Definition of Done)
   119	
   120	An initiative is done — able to be bet on — when it has passed the
   121	cold reviewer's screen against its fitness set, which requires the
   122	feasibility verdict present and the measure with a current condition
   123	and a target. **Consequence on failure:** it stays `proposed` with the
   124	criterion named; no feature is made from it.

    15	# Fitness set: initiative
    16	
    17	An initiative is the product-level problem artifact the PM role makes:
    18	the framing, the measure, the appetite and no-gos, the feasibility and
    19	usability attachments, and the decomposition. These scenarios are the
    20	criteria set the `initiative-check` process (pending — not yet
    21	authored) screens an initiative against; the typedef says why that
    22	screen is the check of record. Evaluated by the `cold-reviewer` role,
    23	never executed.
    24	The judge reads only the criteria set and the initiative; every fact a
    25	scenario judges is one the initiative must carry. The judge's model
    26	and prompt version are recorded with each round verdict.
    27	
    28	## Scenarios

    34	  taken to be worth solving and the outcome it serves, and names the

    36	
    37	Scenario 2: the outcome has a measure, a current condition, and a target

    39	  When it is read
    40	  Then it names who has the problem, one measure with its current

    43

    46	  When it is read
    47	  Then it states a bound in time or capacity, and every no-go carries
    48	  its reason
    49	
    50	Scenario 4: the problem, not the solution
    51	  Given the Framing, For whom, and Appetite sections

    12	# Guideline: initiative
    13	
    14	**Voice principle.** Write the initiative for the authority who must
    15	bet on it in one reading and for the PO role who must make features
    16	from it with no one to ask: the problem in the originator's words, one
    17	measure, a bound, what it will not do — and nothing about how.
    18	
    19	**Highlights (the layer compiled into generating context):** the
    20	originator quoted, the problem and outcome stated, the contract named
    21	· one measure, its current condition quantified, its target · an
    22	appetite in time or capacity; every no-go with a reason · no
    23	technology, structure, or form · feasibility attached; usability attached or
    24	asked · contexts, relationship kinds, and the cross-context flow, or
    25	"not yet" · one page.
    26	
    27	**Layers:** this guideline adds initiative rules on top of the
    28	[base writing style](base-writing-style.md); the base always applies
    29	and is never overridden. When rules conflict, an approved principle
    30	beats the [initiative typedef](../artifacts/initiative.md), which beats
    31	this guideline. Every rule feeds the
    32	[initiative fitness set](../fitness/initiative.fitness.md), scored by
    33	the cold reviewer in the `initiative-check` process (pending).
    34	
    35	---
    36	
    37	## Rules
    38	
    39	**1. Quote the originator; state the problem, the outcome, the contract.**
    40	Before: "Operators have asked for better visibility into failures."
    41	After: "Originator (operator, 2026-08-20, through the operational
    42	contract): 'I open every run to find the one that failed.' Problem:
    43	failed runs are not distinguishable from the run list. Outcome: a
    44	failed run is noticed within one glance."
    45	*Test:* read the Framing section. *Criterion:* the originator's words
    46	are quoted; the problem, the outcome, and the contract are each
    47	stated. *Decision:* yes/no per initiative.
    48	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 1.
    49	
    50	**2. Who, one measure, a current condition, a target, the types.**
    51	Before: "Success: operators are happier with the run list."
    52	After: "For: operators. Measure: time from a run's failure to an
    53	operator noticing it. Now: median 40 minutes. Target: under one
    54	minute. Interaction types: cli, gui."
    55	*Test:* read the For whom section. *Criterion:* who; exactly one
    56	measure, its current condition quantified, its target stated; the
    57	interaction types or "none" with a reason.
    58	*Decision:* yes/no per initiative.
    59	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 2.
    60	
    61	**3. Bound the appetite; reason every no-go.**
    62	Before: "We'll do what it takes; notifications are out of scope."
    63	After: "Appetite: two weeks of the reporting shop. No-gos: notifying
    64	on failure — a separate initiative (alerting) serves it; historical
    65	failure analysis — no originator has asked."
    66	*Test:* read the Appetite section. *Criterion:* a bound in time or
    67	capacity is stated; every no-go carries its reason. *Decision:* yes/no
    68	per initiative.
    69	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 3.
    70	
    71	**4. No solution.**
    72	Before: "Add a status column backed by the failures table and a `--failed`
    73	flag."
    74	After: "The outcome holds on the command line and the graphical
    75	interaction; how a failure becomes visible is the shops'."
    76	*Test:* in the Framing, For whom, and Appetite sections, list every
    77	sentence naming a technology, structure, or interface form.
    78	*Criterion:* the list is empty; an interaction type named is a what. *Decision:* yes/no per initiative.
    79	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 4.
    80	
    81	**5. Attach feasibility; attach usability or ask.**
    82	Before: "Feasibility: to be confirmed."
    83	After: "Feasibility (architect, 2026-08-21): feasible within the
    84	current stack; the run log already records failure. Usability: not yet
    85	— ask to the product designer role, default 'a hypothesis stands until
    86	the first user test'."
    87	*Test:* read the Feasibility and usability section. *Criterion:* the
    88	verdict with reasons is present; where §2 names an interaction type,
    89	the usability evidence or hypothesis is present or marked "not yet"
    90	with the ask that requests it. *Decision:* yes/no per
    91	initiative.
    92	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 5.
    93	
    94	**6. Name the contexts and the flow, or "not yet".**
    95	Before: "Touches reporting and maybe export."
    96	After: "Decomposition (architect): reporting, export; contract
    97	reporting → export: customer–supplier; cross-context flow: none — each
    98	context reads the run log directly." or "Decomposition: not yet."
    99	*Test:* read the Decomposition section. *Criterion:* contexts,
   100	relationship kinds, and the flow or "none" are stated, or the section
   101	says "not yet". *Decision:* yes/no per initiative.
   102	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 6.
   103	
   104	**7. One page.**
   105	Before: a twelve-section document with a history of the reporting
   106	area.
   107	After: six sections within 500 words, the bet readable from the
   108	first two.
   109	*Test:* count words outside the Document History; read the first two
   110	sections alone. *Criterion:* at most 500 words (the typedef's rule);
   111	the bet is statable from the Framing and For whom sections. *Decision:*
   112	yes/no per initiative.
   113	*Derived check:* judged — initiative fitness scenario 7.

    15	## On the check of record and `define-good-up-front`
    16	
    17	The principle's letter is met: the definition of good (the fitness set) is stated up front, it drives both the writing (guideline highlights) and the check, and the check sits with the cold reviewer — a different role with a different accountability (the round's verdict) from the PM who makes and the authority who bets. The feasibility attachment adds a second independent role on the one risk it covers.
    18	
    19	Two things stop it from being a check in fact rather than in name:
    20	
    21	1. **No process carries the screen.** The typedef says "screened by the cold reviewer"; the fitness set says "the judge's model and prompt version are recorded with each round verdict"; the commitment says done "when it has passed the cold reviewer's screen." No process is named that invokes the reviewer on an initiative, applies the "stays `proposed` with the criterion named" consequence, records the round, or declares an exit. `po-output-check` does not fit — its maker is `lead-po`, its `framing` input is the initiative's own first section, and the initiative is not among its inputs. Under `no-orphan-activities` the check of record is presently an orphan activity. Required: name the carrying process, existing or pending, in the typedef's Produced-by — for example an `initiative-check` process mirroring `po-output-check` with maker `lead-pm` (agent-assisted), screener `cold-reviewer`, decider the authority.
    22	2. **What the check covers should be said plainly.** The fitness set checks the initiative's completeness and form — originator quoted, one measure, bound, no solution, attachments. It does not and cannot check whether the problem is worth solving; the same person frames and bets, and the typedef's "check of record since the authority holds the PM role in person" reads as if that gap were closed. It is not, by design. State that the value judgment stands unchecked and rests on the recorded originator words and the measure — then the principle is satisfied for what the definition of good actually defines, and no reader takes the screen for more than it is.

    24	## `basis/artifacts/initiative.md` — findings
    25	
    26	**1. Status lifecycle: three of five transitions have no carrying process, and one names a process that does not do what is claimed.** (§Required frontmatter)
    27	
    28	- `proposed` — "the PM role, on drafting." The discovery-conversation process (linked from lead-pm) is the evident writer; the typedef names no process and does not link it.
    29	- `planned` — "the authority's go/no-go in a review." No process named. The Rules say the go/no-go "is carried by a product decision record linked from the Document History" — see finding 3.
    30	- `active` — "the PO output check's record step, on the first feature derived from it passing." The `po-output-check` v4 record step writes the **feature's** status (`checked`/`returned`/`pending-definition`); the initiative is not among its inputs or outputs, so it cannot write `active`. Either amend that process (and say the amendment is pending) or move the writer.
    31	- `completed` — "the `reconcile-and-close` process." Exists (the architect role links it); not linked here, and whether it records "the measure against its target" I could not verify from the allowed reads.
    32	- `cancelled` — "the PM role's decision in a review … from any state." No process named. "From any state" includes `completed`; bound it to the non-terminal states.
    33	
    34	Required: for each value, name the process (path) that writes it, or mark it "pending — process not yet authored"; link the ones that exist.
    35	
    36	**2. The Commitment and section 4 disagree on whether feasibility may be "not yet".** (§Required sections 4, §Commitment)
    37	
    38	Section 4: "each attached before the go/no-go or marked 'not yet' with the ask that requests it" — feasibility included. Commitment: done "when … its feasibility verdict is attached." Fitness 5 and guideline rule 5 follow section 4 and pass a feasibility "not yet". So an initiative can screen clean and still not be done, with no criterion named — contradicting the consequence clause "stays `proposed` with the criterion named." Required: split the two — feasibility must be present (fitness 5 fails on "not yet"); usability may be "not yet" with its ask. Also drop "before the go/no-go" — sequencing belongs to the carrying process.
    39	
    40	**3. "Product decision record" is undefined and its writer conflicts with `po-output-check`.** (§Rules, last bullet)
    41	
    42	"A decision the initiative records — the go/no-go, a cancellation — is carried by a product decision record linked from the Document History." The term is in no glossary and no typedef. Where it appears (`po-output-check` use-when, lead-pm Accountable) it is **PO output checked by the PM** — so this rule has the authority's bet carried in an artifact the PO makes and the PM checks. Required: either define the record and its writer, or drop the bullet and let the Document History state entry carry the decision with its reasons, as the primer's decision practice already does.
    43	
    44	**4. Scenario-4 scope and the one-page bound have no home in the typedef.** (§Rules, §Derived review checklist)
    45
