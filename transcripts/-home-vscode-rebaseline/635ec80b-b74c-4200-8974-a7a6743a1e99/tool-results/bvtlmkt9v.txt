    15	# Process: Discovery conversation
    16	
    17	**Purpose:** Conduct a bounded discovery dialogue in a declared form —
    18	brainstorm (the first form), interview, or review of evidence: the
    19	authority explores direction with the lead-pm as interlocutor, nothing
    20	is operationalized before convergence, the session record anchors the
    21	conversation, and a converged discovery leaves an initiative recorded
    22	`proposed` — or `proposed` and then `cancelled` in the same document
    23	when the request is declined, so the record of the decline survives.
    24	
    25	**Guiding statement:** Engage the authority's statements as an
    26	interlocutor; record and launch only after convergence.
    27	
    28	**Outcomes:**
    29	- O1. No work is launched and no definition is written before the
    30	  authority converges — witnessed by the `route` branches: the handoff
    31	  is reachable only from the authority's own classification.
    32	- O2. The conversation closes onto a validated session record carrying
    33	  its produced and revised lists — witnessed by the `handoff`
    34	  sub-process, whose child validates the record before landing it.
    35	- O3. Only the authority converges, closes, or cancels — witnessed by
    36	  the `route` branches.
    37	- O4. An inactive conversation holds instead of dangling — witnessed by
    38	  `hold-after` and the run lifecycle.
    39	- O5. A converged discovery returns an initiative recorded `proposed`
    40	  (or `proposed` then `cancelled` with the authority's reason when the
    41	  request is declined); a close without convergence lands the session
    42	  record and frames nothing — witnessed by `route-frame`, which
    43	  reaches `frame` only from the authority's converge classification.
    44	
    45	**Roles:** product-authority (human-held role — explores, converges, and owns
    46	the exclusive right to close or cancel). lead-pm — held by the same
    47	person; its agent steps assist: `engage` prepares the reflection —
    48	probes, names tensions, offers options with a recommendation, keeps the
    49	draft record's quotes and state current — and the authority decides
    50	what converges. The agent operationalizes nothing.

   124	    prompt: |

   126	      to engage, a question, a convergence (the direction is settled —

   133	    name: Route on the input

   135	    inputs: [classification]

   140	      - label: "cancel exit: authority cancels"

   143	      - else: engage

   146	    name: Engage as interlocutor
   147	    run-by: {role: lead-pm, execution: agent}

   150	    checks:

   152	    prompt: |
   153	      Engage the statement as an interlocutor, shaped to the declared
   154	      form — brainstorm: diverge into options before any convergence;
   155	      interview: draw out and quote the originator's own words; review
   156	      of evidence: read the record against its sources. Probe, reflect
   157	      back, name tensions with what is already decided, and offer
   158	      options with a recommendation when a decision is near. Do not
   159	      operationalize — no launching work, no writing definitions, no
   160	      dispatches — before the authority converges. Capture select
   161	      quotes into the draft session record, and maintain
   162	      initiative_draft — the initiative's Framing, For whom, and

   164	      dialogue moves.

   192	      Framing with the originator quoted, For whom with one measure,

   202	    next: close-out
   203

   205	    name: Close the work item
   206	    run-by: {execution: runtime}

   213	    name: Cancel the conversation
   214	    run-by: {execution: runtime}

   222	discovery that produced nothing durable is recorded as exactly that,
   223	never released silently.

    22	# Process: Initiative check
    23	
    24	**Purpose:** Take a proposed initiative to the bet: the solutions
    25	architect and product designer roles attach the sections they own, the
    26	cold reviewer screens the whole against the initiative fitness set —
    27	the check of record on the PM role's framing — and the authority
    28	decides, from the verdict, whether to spend the appetite.
    29	
    30	**Guiding statement:** The bet is taken on the screen's verdict and
    31	the initiative's own first three sections, never on advocacy; a
    32	finding in another role's attachment is that role's to answer, not the
    33	reviser's to rewrite.
    34	
    35	**Outcomes:**
    36	- O1. The screen reads an initiative whose Feasibility and usability
    37	  and Decomposition sections were written by the roles that own them —
    38	  witnessed by `attach-architecture` and `attach-usability` preceding
    39	  `screen`, each outputting the initiative.
    40	- O2. Every round is screened in a fresh context against the
    41	  initiative fitness set and logged — witnessed by `screen`'s
    42	  `fresh-context` and inputs, and by `log-round`.
    43	- O3. The screen loop exits on a clean verdict, on findings no
    44	  criterion names, or at the round cap — witnessed by `route-screen`'s
    45	  labeled branches.
    46	- O4. The decision — bet, hold, or cancel — is the authority's alone,
    47	  taken in a human step; a bet is available only on a screen the
    48	  typedef's commitment admits, and the status the run writes follows
    49	  the decision — witnessed by `decide`'s prompt and outputs and
    50	  `record`'s inputs.
    51	- O5. A repair that touches another role's attachment leaves the run
    52	  as an ask to that role, with a default — witnessed by `revise`'s
    53	  `asks` and the `ask` value.
    54	
    55	**Roles:** attacher (architecture) —
    56	[`../roles/lead-solutions-architect.md`](../roles/lead-solutions-architect.md)
    57	(writes the feasibility verdict and the decomposition; its feasibility
    58	accountability). attacher (usability) —
    59	[`../roles/lead-product-designer.md`](../roles/lead-product-designer.md)
    60	(writes the usability evidence or hypothesis where an interaction type
    61	is named). screener —
    62	[`../roles/cold-reviewer.md`](../roles/cold-reviewer.md) (fresh
    63	context every round; the check of record — the
    64	[initiative typedef](../artifacts/initiative.md) says why no other
    65	role checks the PM role's framing). the authority —
    66	product-authority (human-held; takes the bet). the PM role —
    67	[`../roles/lead-pm.md`](../roles/lead-pm.md) (held by the authority in
    68	person; its agent steps assist — `revise` repairs and asks, `record`
    69	writes the outcome).

    71	**Carried by:**
    72	[`../skills/initiative-check/SKILL.md`](../skills/initiative-check/SKILL.md)

   148	## Steps
   149	
   150	```yaml
   151	start: attach-architecture
   152	parameters: [initiative, criteria_path, contracts, repository, experience_principles, core_tasks]
   153	result: initiative
   154	steps:
   155	  - id: attach-architecture
   156	    name: Attach feasibility and decomposition
   157	    run-by: {role: lead-solutions-architect, execution: agent}
   158	    inputs: [initiative, contracts, repository]
   159	    outputs: [initiative]
   160	    prompt: |
   161	      Read the initiative. Write into its Decomposition section the
   162	      Bounded Contexts it touches, the relationship kind of each
   163	      contract between them it relies on, and the cross-context flow —
   164	      the saga or process manager that will carry it, or "none" when
   165	      the contexts need no flow between them. Write into its
   166	      Feasibility and usability section your feasibility verdict with
   167	      its reasons. Judge from the contracts at contracts and the
   168	      feature repository at repository — your role's admissible
   169	      evidence, never a context's internals. Infeasible is a verdict:
   170	      give it with reasons rather than withholding one. Return the
   171	      initiative.
   172	    next: attach-usability
   173	
   174	  - id: attach-usability
   175	    name: Attach usability evidence
   176	    run-by: {role: lead-product-designer, execution: agent}
   177	    inputs: [initiative, experience_principles, core_tasks]
   178	    outputs: [initiative]
   179	    prompt: |
   180	      Read the initiative. Where its For whom section names an
   181	      interaction type, write into the Feasibility and usability
   182	      section the usability evidence for the outcome on those types,
   183	      or the hypothesis it stands on, or "not yet" with the text of
   184	      the ask that requests it — judged against the experience
   185	      principle set at experience_principles and the core-task list at
   186	      core_tasks, your role's standard. Where
   187	      the For whom section says "none", write that no usability
   188	      attachment is due, with the section's reason. Return the
   189	      initiative.
   190	    next: screen
   191	
   192	  - id: screen
   193	    name: Screen against the fitness set
   194	    run-by: {role: cold-reviewer, execution: agent, fresh-context: true}
   195	    inputs: [initiative, criteria_path]
   196	    outputs: [review, judge_stamp]
   197	    prompt: |
   198	      State, as judge_stamp, the model and prompt version you judge
   199	      as. Read the criteria set at criteria_path and the initiative at
   200	      initiative — nothing else. Judge the initiative against every
   201	      scenario. Report every finding with the criterion it fails by
   202	      name, the quoted text, the change, and whether you could decide
   203	      it (confident) or not (wobbly); for a wobbly finding quote the
   204	      whole passage, since the authority decides from the review. A
   205	      defect no criterion names is a finding with criterion
   206	      "uncovered". Verdict "clean" only if there are no findings;
   207	      otherwise "findings" with the top three changes.
   208	    next: log-round
   209	    annotations:
   210	      fabro: {model: high-reasoning, node: separate-context-per-round}
   211	
   212	  - id: log-round
   213	    name: Record the round
   214	    run-by: {execution: runtime}
   215	    inputs: [review, round_log, judge_stamp, judge_log]
   216	    set:
   217	      round_log: round_log + [review]
   218	      judge_log: judge_log + [judge_stamp]
   219	    next: route-screen
   220	
   221	  - id: route-screen
   222	    name: Route on the screen
   223	    run-by: {execution: runtime}
   224	    inputs: [review, round, round_cap]
   225	    branches:
   226	      - label: "success exit: clean"
   227	        when: review.verdict == "clean"
   228	        next: decide
   229	      - label: "definition exit: every finding is uncovered — nothing a repair can reach"
   230	        when: size(review.findings) > 0 && review.findings.all(f, f.criterion == "uncovered")
   231	        next: decide
   232	      - label: "failsafe exit: round >= round_cap — decide with findings open"
   233	        when: round >= round_cap
   234	        next: decide
   235	      - else: revise
   236	
   237	  - id: revise
   238	    name: Revise the initiative
   239	    run-by: {role: lead-pm, execution: agent}
   240	    inputs: [initiative, review, ask]
   241	    outputs: [initiative]
   242	    asks: [lead-solutions-architect, lead-product-designer]
   243	    prompt: |
   244	      Assist step. Repair every finding whose criterion is named and
   245	      whose passage the PM role owns — the Framing, For whom,
   246	      Appetite, and Features sections. A finding in the Feasibility
   247	      and usability or Decomposition sections is another role's

    23	
    24	**Purpose:** Turn a checked feature into work the Bounded Context shops
    25	can take up: the solutions architect role tags each scenario with the
    26	context that owns its behavior, reads each tagged context's
    27	pre-state, sweeps the feature repository for conflicts, records the
    28	feature as assigned, and sends each shop its scenarios as an
    29	`assign_scenarios` message.
    30	
    31	**Guiding statement:** The decomposition decides, not the wording.
    32	Which context owns a behavior is read from the decomposition and the
    33	contexts' contracts; a scenario no context can own is the architect's
    34	finding, not a guess.

    21	
    22	# Process: PO output check
    23	
    24	**Purpose:** Check a piece of the PO role's output against the PM
    25	role's framing under criteria the definitions state, so that the PM
    26	role decides from a screen verdict — pass, fail with the criterion
    27	named, or a definition change — and never by reading every artifact.
    28	
    29	**Guiding statement:** The check is the criteria applied, not the
    30	checker's reading. A finding the criteria cannot name is a missing
    31	criterion, and the decision that follows is a definition change, not a
    32	verdict on the maker.
    33	
    34	**Outcomes:**
    35	- O1. The output — a feature, a product decision record, or a backlog
    36	  order — is screened against a named criteria set and the framing it
    37	  claims to serve — witnessed by `screen`'s inputs and the
    38	  `screen-review` it returns.
    39	- O2. The PM role decides from the review and round log; the artifact
    40	  reaches the PM role only through the findings' quotes — witnessed by
    41	  `decide`'s inputs, which exclude the artifact.
    42	- O3. Every screen round is recorded and the loop exits on a clean
    43	  verdict, on findings no criterion names, or at the round cap —
    44	  witnessed by `log-round` and `route-screen`.
    45	- O4. A fail names the criterion missed; a definition change names the
    46	  gap, and the gap is filed in the named definition's Document History
    47	  — witnessed by the `check-decision` fields and `record`'s
    48	  `definition` and `gap_entry` outputs.
    49	- O5. A question the framing cannot answer leaves the run as an ask to
    50	  the PM role, with a default, and the run resumes — witnessed by
    51	  `revise`'s `asks` and the `ask` value.
    52	- O6. A feature's first pass activates its planned initiative — the
    53	  `active` status the initiative typedef assigns this process, written
    54	  only over `planned` — witnessed by `record`'s `initiative` output
    55	  and its prompt.
    56	
    57	**Roles:** maker — [`../roles/lead-po.md`](../roles/lead-po.md)
    58	(submits and revises; never decides its own pass). screener —
    59	[`../roles/cold-reviewer.md`](../roles/cold-reviewer.md) (fresh
    60	context every round; judges against the criteria). the PM role —
    61	[`../roles/lead-pm.md`](../roles/lead-pm.md) (accountable for the
    62	decision; a human-held role that decides from the verdict; its
    63	assisting agent prepares the record and the gap entry).

    66	[`../skills/po-output-check/SKILL.md`](../skills/po-output-check/SKILL.md)

   147	    outputs: [review, judge_stamp]

   152	      against every criterion and against the framing: does each part
   153	      of it trace to the framing, and does anything in it serve no
   154	      framed outcome? Report every finding with the criterion it fails
   155	      by name — "framing" for a part that does not trace to the framing
   156	      or serves no framed outcome — the quoted text, the change, and
   157	      whether you could decide it (confident) or not (wobbly); for a
   158	      wobbly finding quote the whole passage, since the PM role decides
   159	      from the review alone. A defect no criterion names is a finding
   160	      with criterion "uncovered". Verdict "clean"
   161	      only if there are no findings; otherwise "findings" with the top
   162	      three changes.

   185	        when: size(review.findings) > 0 && review.findings.all(f, f.criterion == "uncovered")
   186	        next: decide
   187	      - label: "failsafe exit: round >= round_cap — decide with findings open"
   188	        when: round >= round_cap
   189	        next: decide
   190	      - else: revise
   191	
   192	  - id: revise
   193	    name: Revise the output
   194	    run-by: {role: lead-po, execution: agent}

   199	      Repair every finding whose criterion is named, "framing"
   200	      included; leave findings marked "uncovered" as they are — they

   246	      judge's model and prompt version, then a state entry carrying the decision

   248	      "returned" on fail with the criterion named, "pending-definition"
