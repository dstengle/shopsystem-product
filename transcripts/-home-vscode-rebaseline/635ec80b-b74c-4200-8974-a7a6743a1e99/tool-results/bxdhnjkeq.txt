    13	# Research report: a cold read of the whole system against the product operating model
    14	
    15	## Executive summary
    16	
    17	*How to read the labels.* **High** — the claim is read directly from
    18	quoted basis text and judged against a standard the shop's own research
    19	reports carry; **medium** — an inference across several documents, or a
    20	judgment of fit; **low** — a prediction about how the system will
    21	behave in use, which no run has yet shown. Confidence is about the
    22	evidence; where a finding predicts, likelihood is stated in its own
    23	phrase.
    24	
    25	*How it was read.* Three readers in fresh contexts read the corpus
    26	with different lenses — the product operating model, end-to-end
    27	coherence, and a product leader's first hour — against a stated
    28	standard, not a persona: the shop's research-prompting report found
    29	expert personas do not improve accuracy, so each reader was given the
    30	product operating model as the five research reports document it
    31	(Cagan, Torres, Perri, Shape Up, Amazon, Team Topologies, DDD) and the
    32	shop's nine working principles.
    33	
    34	*Vocabulary.* The *basis* is the definition corpus on `rebaseline`;
    35	a *hop* is one hand-off in a feature's path from request to shipped
    36	change, and its *carrier* is the process or step defined to perform
    37	it; a *making hop* produces an artifact (a framing, a brief), as
    38	against a checking or carrying hop; the three readers are named by
    39	lens — R1 the operating-model reader, R2 the coherence reader, R3 the
    40	cold-arrival reader; "product-process report" is
    41	`research/product-process-2026-08.md`, and citations of the form
    42	"report F6" name that report's finding, not this one's; *framing* is the PM role's recorded statement of a request's
    43	problem and outcome; the *initiative* is the product-level artifact
    44	the product-process report proposes; a *BC-shop* owns one Bounded
    45	Context; the *router* is a role name `reconcile-and-close` uses.
    46	
    47	1. **The verdict, in the coherence reader's words: "a well-built
    48	   checking apparatus around an empty making pipeline."** Tracing one
    49	   feature through the defined chain, four of ten hops have a defined
    50	   carrier — two checks (the PO output check, the conformance check),
    51	   one carrying hop (`reconcile-and-close`), and the discovery
    52	   conversation, which produces a session record (a conversation's
    53	   anchor, not a chain artifact) but not the framing the next hop
    54	   needs. No hop that makes a chain artifact has a carrier. No process produces a framing, authors a
    55	   brief or scenario set, assigns checked work to a shop, dispatches
    56	   it, answers an ask, writes a changelog, or measures an outcome.
    57	   By the corpus's own rule — a definition chain "required before
    58	   instances are authored" — no PO artifact may yet be authored: the
    59	   four types have typedefs and fitness sets but no guideline,
    60	   authoring process, or skill. *(High.)*
    61	2. **The product is never named.** No document on a newcomer's path
    62	   says what the product does or for whom; the cold-arrival reader
    63	   inferred "a system for running AI-agent shops" from the core-task
    64	   list at minute 55. The architecture principles make the design "the
    65	   authoritative statement of what the product is and does," and the
    66	   architect is accountable for a structural model "readable without
    67	   the code" — neither artifact exists. No customer or user appears in
    68	   any discovery process; the only discovery is the authority in
    69	   dialogue with an agent. *(High.)*
    70	3. **The framing — the pivot every check runs against — is the one
    71	   artifact no role checks and no typedef defines.** It is
    72	   `lead-pm`'s exclusive decision ("no role overrides this one's
    73	   framing"), the criterion always named in the PO output check, and a
    74	   required frontmatter link in two of the four PO typedefs (brief,
    75	   acceptance scenarios) — with no typedef,
    76	   status, measure, or history. The role the authority holds in person
    77	   is the only role whose output is exempt from `define-good-up-front`.
    78	   *(High.)*
    79	4. **Three of the four product risks have a named owner; feasibility is
    80	   owned only by reference** — the architect's six accountabilities
    81	   never name it. Viability is the PM's with no evidence source: the
    82	   admissible-evidence list carries nothing on cost or capacity, and the
    83	   one cost signal (the architect's vendor threshold) escalates to the
    84	   authority, not the PM role. *(High for the text; medium for the
    85	   consequence.)*
    86	5. **Nothing measures an outcome after shipping, so `delivery-verified`
    87	   stops at the demonstration and the product is outside
    88	   `feedback-loops-with-consumers`.** The PM's evidence list names
    89	   "measured outcomes of shipped work"; no process produces one;
    90	   `reconcile-and-close` ends at "follow-ups filed." The principles
    91	   measure the shop's machinery and never the product. *(High; the
    92	   operating-model reader's gloss, "the build trap encoded as a
    93	   principle set," is its judgment.)*
    94	6. **The shop is Shape Up-style bounded delegation to component
    95	   teams, and no document says so.** BC-shops receive a problem slice
    96	   plus a co-produced Gherkin set and are done when every scenario has
    97	   a status — bounded scope, fixed done, not an empowered team choosing
    98	   how to reach an outcome. Coherent as a design; unnamed, so a reader
    99	   applies Cagan's empowered-team expectations to the wrong unit;
   100	   for a request that crosses contexts, the product-process
   101	   report's F6 (Fowler/Narayan) gives the cross-team answer: "solution champions" and initiatives "parceled
   102	   out to pre-existing product-mode teams". The
   103	   appetite Shape Up pairs with bounded work is absent from the brief.
   104	   *(Medium — fit judgment.)*
   105	7. **Three principle conflicts the design carries.**
   106	   `least-context` ("nothing ambient") against `CLAUDE.md` loading the
   107	   principles and primer into every session; `single-source-of-truth`
   108	   against the primer as a second, stale statement of state and against
   109	   inline restatements of framing, ask, and the four risks that the
   110	   cold-reviewer's "unintroduced term" standard has been pushing into
   111	   role files; and `define-good-up-front` against one person holding
   112	   `product-authority` and `lead-pm`. The basis names that same-person
   113	   holding but never says the cold-reviewer screen is therefore the
   114	   check of record; only the product-process report does. State it in
   115	   the basis, or the maker/checker split has no home. *(High for the first two; medium for the third.)*
   116	8. **Under-defined where the product is, over-defined where it is
   117	   not.** No product description, context map, decomposition or ADR
   118	   typedef, operational contract for the lead shop (the contract
   119	   `intent-provenance` says intent enters the product through), roadmap, changelog,
   120	   answering step for asks, or `router` role — yet three conversation
   121	   processes with seven-day holds, a 300-line close-out process
   122	   blocking on a tool that "is a spec, not a build," a chain-migration
   123	   process, and three review rounds per definition before any instance
   124	   exists. The migration plan's own Phase 2 rule: "Any definition not
   125	   needed for it is not foundation work." The cold-arrival reader's
   126	   count: ~2,000 lines on how documents are checked, zero describing a
   127	   user, a problem, or an outcome. *(High for the inventory; medium for
   128	   the judgment.)*
   129	9. **No cadence.** The only time constants are `hold-after: P7D` and
   130	   `ask-cap: P1D`; nothing schedules when problems are chosen, bets
   131	   placed, priorities re-read, or users seen. Every loop is
   132	   event-driven from the authority's next message. *(High for the
   133	   observation; low for the prediction that follows — that for one
   134	   person this is the same as no prioritization process — which is
   135	   likely, in use.)*
   136	10. **The ask mechanism's defaults are decisions in disguise for a
   137	    one-person shop.** An ask on scope or intent that goes unanswered
   138	    for a day resolves to the asker's default — so the reserved decision is
   139	    taken by the asking role, the very role the ask exists to keep it
   140	    from; "at most one ask per
   141	    run" presses the asker to guess on the second question; and no
   142	    process defines the answering step for `lead-pm` or the architect.
   143	    Hold-and-resume fits asynchronous fleets; the authority works in
   144	    the interactive position. *(Medium for the reading of the
   145	    definitions; low for the prediction that defaults will silently
   146	    become scope decisions in use — likely, on the one-day cap.)*
   147	11. **What a product leader would copy — the reasons to keep the
   148	    apparatus.** The PM decides from a fresh-context screen verdict,
   149	    never from the artifact, so the backlog-administrator failure is
   150	    designed out; the "definition-change" exit routes an unnamed defect
   151	    to the standard, not the maker — the one feedback loop with a
   152	    named consumer; every loop declares its exit; decisions live only
   153	    as Document History in the changed artifact; the decision-brief
   154	    discipline (answer first, asks with defaults, a budget, an
   155	    independent cold read) is "the best-designed decision path in the
   156	    corpus"; the lead-pm anti-rationalization list is "the sharpest
   157	    writing in the corpus"; provenance end to end; usability owned.
   158	    *(High that they are present; medium that they are rare.)*
   159	12. **The product-process proposal, re-read by these lenses.** It
   160	    fills the artifact gaps (strategy, initiative, roadmap, changelog)
   161	    but as written moves the writing of the problem, its measure,
   162	    appetite, and no-gos to the role whose exclusive domain is backlog
   163	    order — the hollowed-out PO of the PM/PO research inverted into a
   164	    hollowed-out PM — and still leaves the framing unscreened and the
   165	    outcome unmeasured. The principle-compatible form: the PM makes
   166	    the initiative (absorbing the framing), the cold reviewer screens
   167	    it against a fitness set, the authority bets in a review — the
   168	    pattern the proposal already uses for the strategy. *(Medium.)*
   169	13. **The judgment — the smallest set of changes that makes one
   170	    feature traceable end to end, and what to defer.** Five changes and
   171	    two repairs, not the dozen the proposal lists: (a) an `initiative` typedef with a status, one outcome
   172	    measure, an appetite, and the framing absorbed as its first section
   173	    — made by `lead-pm`, screened by the cold reviewer, bet by the
   174	    authority; (b) one PO authoring process that ends at the PO output
   175	    check, its skill the compiled rendering — which leaves each PO type
   176	    still without a guideline, so accepting (b) means the authority
   177	    accepts the chain rule unmet for the four PO types during Phase 2's
   178	    first run, recorded in their Document History — a yes/no of its
   179	    own; (c) an
   180	    assignment step the architect runs, carrying checked scenarios to
   181	    a shop; (d) a changelog-and-measure step in `reconcile-and-close`
   182	    that closes the initiative's outcome; (e) the first paragraph the
   183	    primer lacks — what the product is, for whom, what phase we are in
   184	    — before Phase 2 starts, since every reader stumbled on its absence
   185	    first. Plus two one-line repairs: a `router` role, and feasibility
   186	    named in the architect's accountabilities. Defer until a run needs
   187	    them: the three conversation processes' hold machinery, close-out,
   188	    chain-migration, and further review rounds on definitions with no
   189	    instance.
   190	14. **Two decisions only the authority can take.** Whether BC-shops are
   191	    component teams by design (name it) or should become
   192	    stream-aligned for some flows (Fowler/Narayan's cross-team
   193	    initiatives with "solution champions"; LeSS's feature teams); and whether the framing gets its check inside the initiative (13(a)
   194	    absorbs it, and the cold reviewer screens the whole) or on its own
   195	    — answered by 13(a) if taken; live only if 13(a) is declined, in
   196	    which case: a fitness set and a screen for the framing, or accept it
   197	    as the one unchecked artifact, stated as such.
   198	
   199	## Method
   200	
   201	Three readers in fresh contexts, each given the frame's standard and
   202	one lens: R1 the product operating model (four risks, problems vs
   203	features, vision→strategy→discovery→delivery); R2 coherence — one
   204	imagined feature ("export a report as CSV from the CLI and the GUI",
   205	crossing two contexts) traced hop by hop through every defined process
   206	and artifact, with principle conflicts and orphan activities recorded;
   207	R3 cold arrival — the primer and README first, then only what they
   208	link, sixteen documents in one hour, ending at one concrete decision
   209	("approve a brief"). Each returned quoted basis text with path and
   210	heading. Sources: the basis on `rebaseline` at commit `08fe8a3`, the
   211	five research reports the frame named — a sixth, the
   212	solutions-architect report, was not opened (the product-process report
   213	read from
   214	`origin/research`, since the local `research` ref lags the remote —
   215	noted by one reader), and `main:drafts/migration-plan.md`. No web
   216	source was opened; standards are cited through the reports that carry
   217	them. An independent verification pass reopened the basis files
   218	behind every quote.

   220	## Findings
   221	
   222	### F1 — The making pipeline is undefined *(high)*
   223	
   224	- R2's trace of ten hops: "Of ten hops, four have a defined carrier.
   225	  Two of those four are the check steps; the making steps between them
   226	  are undefined."
   227	- No framing producer: `discovery-conversation` produces only
   228	  `session_record` (`$ref: session-record, from:
   229	  pkg:shopsystem-knowledge/session-record`); grep of `basis/processes/`
   230	  for "framing" outside `po-output-check` returns nothing;
   231	  `po-output-check` takes `framing: {type: string, format:
   232	  uri-reference}`.
   233	- `no-orphan-activities`: "Every activity in the system MUST be part of
   234	  a defined process"; `lead-po` is "the role that makes the
   235	  requirements" and no process has a brief, scenario set, or order among
   236	  its outputs; `po-output-check` begins with `parameters: [artifact,
   237	  framing, criteria_path]` — the artifact already exists.
   238	- The chain rule: "definition chain — the six linked definitions of
   239	  good for one artifact type; required before instances are authored"
   240	  (glossary); the four PO types hold typedef and fitness set only.
   241	- Assignment: the architect is accountable for "Scenario assignment:
   242	  every accepted scenario mapped to the Bounded Context that owns it";
   243	  no process defines assignment, message composition, or dispatch; the
   244	  BC side arrives as foreign types (`pkg:shopsystem-messaging`,
   245	  `pkg:shopsystem-knowledge`, `pkg:beads`) on a branch whose primer
   246	  says "Nothing exists here except through an explicit import step."
   247	- `reconcile-and-close` runs steps as `run-by: {role: router,
   248	  execution: agent}`; `basis/roles/` holds no `router.md`; "Carried by:
   249	  the existing `reconcile-and-close` skill + executable wrapper" —
   250	  `basis/skills/reconcile-and-close/` exists and is empty; the discrepancy branch files a
   251	  task and ends, with no consuming process.
   252	- Asks: "Answering is an activity of the answering role… those steps
   253	  are defined in the processes that answer, not here"
   254	  (process-definition typedef); no process defines an answering step
   255	  for `lead-pm` or `lead-solutions-architect`.
   256	
   257	### F2 — The product is unnamed; discovery has no customer *(high)*
   258	
   259	- R3: "No document on the newcomer's path states what the product does
   260	  or for whom"; the primer opens "This is the `rebaseline` branch: the
   261	  greenfield tree of the shopsystem-product migration"; the README
   262	  opens "The `basis/` tree is the shop's definition corpus"; the
   263	  product's substance appears only in `basis/experience/core-tasks.md`
   264	  ("start a run", "answer an ask", "raise a clarify").
   265	- `architecture-principles.md` §bidirectional-conformance: "The design
   266	  — descriptions, contracts, recorded decisions — MUST be the
   267	  authoritative statement of what the product is and does";
   268	  `lead-solutions-architect.md`: "The structural model of the product,
   269	  maintained as an artifact readable without the code" — no such
   270	  artifact in the tree.
   271	- `discovery-conversation.md` §Roles: "product-authority… lead-pm — held
   272	  by the same person; its agent steps assist"; the migration plan's
   273	  Phase 2: "the PM interviews the authority about the
   274	  progressive-disclosure feature"; `lead-product-designer.md` may
   275	  decide "what user research runs" — no process runs any.
   276	- R1: the architect and designer are absent from the discovery process
   277	  though both roles' interfaces and the PM/PO research place feasibility
   278	  and usability "in discovery rather than after it"; `lead-pm`'s
   279	  anti-rationalization "'It's infeasible, so the framing is wrong.' →
   280	  Infeasible returns the problem for re-framing" confirms a post-hoc
   281	  loop.
   282	
   283	### F3 — The framing is unchecked and untyped *(high)*

   294	- `principles.md` §define-good-up-front: "The check MUST sit with a

   296	- Product-process report, item 1: the framing "carries no status,
   297	  measure, or history."
   298

   315	### F5 — No outcome is measured after shipping *(high)*

   366	- `principles.md` §single-source-of-truth remedy: "Writers link or

   383	### F8 — Over- and under-definition *(high / medium)*

   409	### F9 — Cadence and the ask mechanism *(medium)*
   410	
   411	- Time constants: `hold-after: P7D` in seven processes; `ask-cap: P1D`
   412	  in two; every conversation loop returns to an `observe` step run by
   413	  `product-authority`.
   414	- `process-definition.md` §Run lifecycle: "An unanswered ask resolves
   415	  to its `default` at the process's `ask-cap`… and the run resumes on
   416	  the default"; "A step returns at most one ask per run — the loop's
