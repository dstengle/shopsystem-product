    22	# Process: PO output check
    23	
    24	**Purpose:** Check a piece of the PO role's output against the PM
    25	role's framing under criteria the definitions state, so that the PM
    26	role decides from a screen verdict — pass, fail with the criterion
    27	named, or a definition change — and never by reading every artifact.
    28	
    29	**Guiding statement:** The check is the criteria applied, not the
    30	checker's reading. A finding the criteria cannot name is a missing
    31	criterion, and the decision that follows is a definition change, not a
    32	verdict on the maker.
    33	
    34	**Outcomes:**
    35	- O1. The output — a feature, a product decision record, or a backlog
    36	  order — is screened against a named criteria set and the framing it
    37	  claims to serve — witnessed by `screen`'s inputs and the
    38	  `screen-review` it returns.
    39	- O2. The PM role decides from the review and round log; the artifact
    40	  reaches the PM role only through the findings' quotes — witnessed by
    41	  `decide`'s inputs, which exclude the artifact.
    42	- O3. Every screen round is recorded and the loop exits on a clean
    43	  verdict, on findings no criterion names, or at the round cap —
    44	  witnessed by `log-round` and `route-screen`.
    45	- O4. A fail names the criterion missed; a definition change names the
    46	  gap, and the gap is filed in the named definition's Document History
    47	  — witnessed by the `check-decision` fields and `record`'s
    48	  `definition` and `gap_entry` outputs.
    49	- O5. A question the framing cannot answer leaves the run as an ask to
    50	  the PM role, with a default, and the run resumes — witnessed by
    51	  `revise`'s `asks` and the `ask` value.
    52	
    53	**Roles:** maker — [`../roles/lead-po.md`](../roles/lead-po.md)
    54	(submits and revises; never decides its own pass). screener —
    55	[`../roles/cold-reviewer.md`](../roles/cold-reviewer.md) (fresh
    56	context every round; judges against the criteria). the PM role —
    57	[`../roles/lead-pm.md`](../roles/lead-pm.md) (accountable for the
    58	decision; a human-held role that decides from the verdict; its
    59	assisting agent prepares the record and the gap entry).
    60	
    61	**Carried by:**
    62	[`../skills/po-output-check/SKILL.md`](../skills/po-output-check/SKILL.md)
    63	— generated from this definition by
    64	[`../tools/compile_process.py`](../tools/compile_process.py), never
    65	edited by hand.

    96	## Data
    97	
    98	Each entry names a process-local value. Simple types use JSON Schema
    99	names inline; every structured shape is a `$ref` to a defined type with
   100	an explicit source. Conditions are CEL expressions over these names. A
   101	*criteria set* is the approved fitness set or guideline for the
   102	artifact's type. The framing is always a criterion, named `framing`;
   103	until a criteria set exists for a type, `criteria_path` names the
   104	framing itself and `framing` is the only named criterion. The artifact
   105	status values this process sets — `checked` on pass, `returned` on
   106	fail, `pending-definition` on definition change — are this process's
   107	until the artifact typedefs define their own.
   108

   124	## Steps
   125	
   126	```yaml
   127	start: screen
   128	parameters: [artifact, framing, criteria_path]
   129	result: decision
   130	steps:
   131	  - id: screen
   132	    name: Screen against the criteria
   133	    run-by: {role: cold-reviewer, execution: agent, fresh-context: true}
   134	    inputs: [artifact, framing, criteria_path]
   135	    outputs: [review]
   136	    prompt: |
   137	      Read the criteria set at criteria_path, the framing at framing,
   138	      and the artifact at artifact — nothing else. Judge the artifact
   139	      against every criterion and against the framing: does each part
   140	      of it trace to the framing, and does anything in it serve no
   141	      framed outcome? Report every finding with the criterion it fails
   142	      by name — "framing" for a part that does not trace to the framing
   143	      or serves no framed outcome — the quoted text, the change, and
   144	      whether you could decide it (confident) or not (wobbly); for a
   145	      wobbly finding quote the whole passage, since the PM role decides
   146	      from the review alone. A defect no criterion names is a finding
   147	      with criterion "uncovered". Verdict "clean"
   148	      only if there are no findings; otherwise "findings" with the top
   149	      three changes.
   150	    next: log-round
   151	    annotations:
   152	      fabro: {model: high-reasoning, node: separate-context-per-round}
   153	
   154	  - id: log-round
   155	    name: Record the round
   156	    run-by: {execution: runtime}
   157	    inputs: [review, round_log]
   158	    set:
   159	      round_log: round_log + [review]
   160	    next: route-screen
   161	
   162	  - id: route-screen
   163	    name: Route on the screen
   164	    run-by: {execution: runtime}
   165	    inputs: [review, round, round_cap]
   166	    branches:
   167	      - label: "success exit: clean"
   168	        when: review.verdict == "clean"
   169	        next: decide
   170	      - label: "definition exit: every finding is uncovered — nothing the maker can repair"
   171	        when: size(review.findings) > 0 && review.findings.all(f, f.criterion == "uncovered")
   172	        next: decide
   173	      - label: "failsafe exit: round >= round_cap — decide with findings open"
   174	        when: round >= round_cap
   175	        next: decide
   176	      - else: revise
   177	
   178	  - id: revise
   179	    name: Revise the output
   180	    run-by: {role: lead-po, execution: agent}
   181	    inputs: [artifact, review, framing, ask]
   182	    outputs: [artifact]
   183	    asks: [lead-pm]
   184	    prompt: |
   185	      Repair every finding whose criterion is named, "framing"
   186	      included; leave findings marked "uncovered" as they are — they
   187	      are the PM role's to decide. If a repair needs something the framing does not say —
   188	      the originator's intent, whether a thing is in scope, a decision
   189	      another role's domain holds — return an ask to lead-pm instead
   190	      of guessing: the question, its kind, the default you will apply
   191	      if unanswered, and a checkpoint holding the repairs made so far.
   192	      On the first pass ask is absent; if it carries an answer or
   193	      resolved defaulted, act on it and finish the repairs.
   194	    next: advance-round
   195	
   196	  - id: advance-round
   197	    name: Advance the round
   198	    run-by: {execution: runtime}
   199	    inputs: [round]
   200	    set:
   201	      round: round + 1
   202	    next: screen
   203	
   204	  - id: decide
   205	    name: Decide on the verdict
   206	    run-by: {role: lead-pm, execution: human}
   207	    inputs: [review, round_log, framing]
   208	    outputs: [decision]
   209	    prompt: |
   210	      From the final review and the round log, decide. "pass": the
   211	      screen is clean, or its only findings are uncovered and you judge
   212	      none of them needs a criterion — say so in the reasons. "fail": a
   213	      named criterion, "framing" included, is still missed at the round
   214	      cap — name it.
   215	      "definition-change": an uncovered finding should have been a
   216	      criterion, or a criterion the output met produced something that
   217	      does not serve the framing — name the definition and what it
   218	      lacks. Where both a missed named criterion and an open uncovered finding
   219	      stand at the cap, "fail" takes precedence and the reasons carry
   220	      the uncovered finding. Decide from the quotes in the review.
   221	      Record your reasons.
   222	    next: record
   223	
   224	  - id: record
   225	    name: Record the decision
   226	    run-by: {role: lead-pm, execution: agent}
   227	    inputs: [decision, artifact, round_log]
   228	    outputs: [artifact, gap_entry, definition]
   229	    prompt: |
   230	      Write into the artifact's Document History one review entry per
   231	      round with its verdict, then a state entry carrying the decision
   232	      and its reasons. Set the artifact's status: "checked" on pass,
   233	      "returned" on fail with the criterion named, "pending-definition"
   234	      on definition-change. On "definition-change", write a review
   235	      entry stating the gap into the Document History of the definition
   236	      the decision names; return that file's path as definition and the
   237	      entry's text as gap_entry. Otherwise return both empty. Return
   238	      the artifact.
   239	    next: end
   240	```

    13	# Artifact type: feature
    14	
    15	## Identity and ancestry
    16	
    17	- **Type:** `feature` — a Gherkin Feature: one capability described
    18	  from the user's or agent's point of view, with the scenarios that
    19	  state, as requirements, what counts as done for it. A product-level
    20	  artifact: it belongs to the [initiative](initiative.md) it is made
    21	  from and may hold scenarios owned by
    22	  several Bounded Contexts. The scenario is the atom; the feature is
    23	  its grouping; the feature repository — the lead shop's directory of
    24	  feature artifacts — is the record of what is specified, and a
    25	  context's assigned set is the subset of scenarios, across all
    26	  features, tagged to it. The instance is a
    27	  markdown document with frontmatter whose Scenarios section holds one
    28	  fenced Gherkin block — the executable unit the shops receive; the
    29	  other sections are the document's, not Gherkin's. Scenarios are
    30	  executed by the owning shops; the feature as a document is judged by
    31	  the [feature fitness set](../fitness/feature.fitness.md), never
    32	  executed there. `ancestry` names no generic root: a feature is
    33	  neither a request nor a definition.
    34	- **Produced by:** the [PO role](../roles/lead-po.md), as sole author —
    35	  the scenarios' steps included; the product designer role contributes
    36	  usability and accessibility criteria, the solutions architect role
    37	  its non-functional constraints where the decomposition names them.
    38	  The owning shops do not co-author: conflicts with behavior already
    39	  specified are caught by the repository sweep at assignment, and a
    40	  shop's objection travels as a clarify or a returned dispatch after
    41	  it receives its scenarios. Checked by the
    42	  [PO output check](../processes/po-output-check.md); its scenarios
    43	  assigned to Bounded Contexts by the solutions architect role in the
    44	  [scenario assignment](../processes/scenario-assignment.md) process.
    45	  **Consumed by:** the shops, each receiving the scenarios tagged to it;
    46	  the [interaction conformance check](../processes/interaction-conformance-check.md)
    47	  for the interaction types named; the changelog rendering the same report proposes, when the
    48	  [reconcile-and-close](../processes/reconcile-and-close.md) process
    49	  closes the work that delivered its scenarios.
    50	
    51	## Required frontmatter
    52	
    53	`type: feature`, `id`, `status`, `version`, `name` (the Feature's
    54	name), `initiative` (link to the initiative it is made from; its
    55	framing is that document's first section), `owner`, `created`,
    56	`updated`. `approved` does not apply: a feature's terminal state is
    57	`assigned`. Status values and their writers: `draft` (the PO role);
    58	`checked`, `returned`, `pending-definition` (the PO output check's
    59	record step, replacing `draft`); a returned or pending feature the PO
    60	role resubmits goes back to `draft`; `assigned` (the scenario
    61	assignment process's record step, replacing `checked` when every
    62	scenario carries its tag); a feature the assignment process returns
    63	goes to `returned`.
    64	
    65	## Required sections
    66	
    67	1. **Feature** — the Gherkin `Feature:` line with its name, and the
    68	   narrative beneath it: who the capability is for, what they can do,
    69	   and the outcome it serves, in the framing's words.
    70	2. **Contributors** — for every scenario, the owning shop named — the
    71	   shop the solutions architect's decomposition places the behavior in
    72	   (ownership for assignment, not authorship); where the capability has
    73	   an interaction, the product designer role's usability acceptance
    74	   criteria and the accessibility criteria; where the decomposition
    75	   names them, the solutions architect role's non-functional
    76	   constraints, riding as criteria on the scenarios they bound.
    77	3. **Interaction types** — always present: the interaction types the
    78	   capability must be available on, from the
    79	   [core-task list](../experience/core-tasks.md), or "none" with the
    80	   reason when the capability has no interaction.
    81	4. **Scenarios** — one fenced `gherkin` block, the executable unit:
    82	   it opens with the `Feature:` line and narrative of §1 verbatim (§1
    83	   is the document's reading of the block's head; the block is the
    84	   source, and the repeat is accepted so the document reads without
    85	   the block), then an optional `Background:` (its Given steps judged as any
    86	   step), then each `Scenario:` carrying the tags `@feature:<id>` (so a
    87	   scenario travels alone to a shop and still names its feature —
    88	   the reason the Feature line's tag inheritance is not relied on),
    89	   `@hash:<sha>` (a hash of the scenario's text), and, once assigned,
    90	   `@bounded-context:<name>`; Given/When/Then with one action or event
    91	   in the When and an outcome observable in the running system in the
    92	   Then; no step names an implementation detail.
    93	5. **Edges** — a table of every failure and boundary case the framing
    94	   or a contributor's criteria name: case · who named it · the
    95	   `Scenario:` name that covers it, or "out of scope" with the reason.
    96	
    97	## Rules
    98	
    99	- A scenario's `@bounded-context:` tag is written by the solutions
   100	  architect role at assignment, never by the PO role — a writer rule
   101	  the assignment process enforces; the check does not judge the tag's
   102	  presence, since a re-verified or resubmitted feature may carry it. In
   103	  the assignment process, a scenario whose owning context differs from
   104	  its Contributors' shop is treated as unowned with that reason, so the
   105	  feature returns (`returned`) for the PO role to correct the owning
   106	  shop it names.
   107	- A changed scenario text is a new scenario with a new `@hash:`.
   108	  Whether the hash matches the text is a mechanical check, to be filed
   109	  as a lint.
   110	- A scenario's conflicts with behavior already specified are caught
   111	  by the sweep of the feature repository at assignment, never by
   112	  asking the shop during authoring; the shop's voice after dispatch
   113	  is the clarify and the return.
   114	
   115	## Commitment (Definition of Done)
   116	
   117	A feature is done when it has passed the PO output check against its
   118	fitness set and the framing, and every scenario carries a
   119	`@bounded-context:` tag. **Consequence on failure:** it is returned
   120	with the criterion named and no scenario is dispatched.
   121	
   122	## Sources
   123	
   124	Gherkin (Feature, narrative, Background, Scenario, tags — the block's
   125	form); the
   126	experience principles `core-task-parity` and `accessible-by-standard`.

    15	# Fitness set: feature
    16	
    17	A feature is a Gherkin Feature — one capability from the user's or
    18	agent's point of view with the scenarios that state what counts as
    19	done for it — authored by the PO role alone, product-level, its
    20	scenarios later assigned to Bounded Contexts by the solutions
    21	architect role.
    22	The scenarios are executable by the owning shops; this set judges the
    23	feature as a document and is never executed. These scenarios are the
    24	criteria set the [PO output check](../processes/po-output-check.md)
    25	screens a feature against, alongside the framing (criterion
    26	`framing`). Evaluated by the `cold-reviewer` role. The judge reads only
    27	the criteria set, the framing, and the feature; a fact the feature must
    28	carry — an owning shop, a tag, an edge — is what these scenarios make it
    29	carry. Assignment is not judged here: the `@bounded-context:` tag is
    30	set after the check.
    31	
    32	## Scenarios
    33	
    34	Scenario 1: each scenario is one observable behavior
    35	  Given a scenario in the feature's Gherkin block, Background steps
    36	  included
    37	  When its steps are read
    38	  Then the When is one action or event, the Then an outcome observable
    39	  in the running system, and no step names an implementation detail
    40	
    41	Scenario 2: ownership and criteria are present
    42	  Given the Contributors section
    43	  When each scenario is read against it
    44	  Then an owning shop is named for that scenario, and, where the
    45	  Interaction types section names a type, the product designer role's
    46	  usability acceptance criteria and the accessibility criteria are
    47	  present, and, where the Contributors section says the decomposition names them,
    48	  the solutions architect role's non-functional constraints are
    49	  present (sources are provenance, not documents to open)
    50	
    51	Scenario 3: identity tags are present
    52	  Given each scenario's tags
    53	  When they are read
    54	  Then `@feature:<id>` and `@hash:<sha>` are present (whether the hash
    55	  matches the text is a lint, not this judge's; the `@bounded-context:`
    56	  tag is assignment's and is not judged here, present or absent)
    57	
    58	Scenario 4: every listed edge is covered
    59	  Given the Edges table and the cases the framing or a contributor's
    60	  criteria name
    61	  When each case is read
    62	  Then it names the covering scenario by its Scenario name or is marked
    63	  out of scope with a reason, and every case the framing or a contributor's criteria name appears
    64	  in the table
    65	
    66	Scenario 5: interaction types are stated
    67	  Given the Interaction types section
    68	  When it is read
    69	  Then it names the types the capability must be available on, or
    70	  "none" with a reason the framing bears out (the experience principle
    71	  `core-task-parity`)
    72	
    73	Scenario 6: the feature says who it is for and why
    74	  Given the Feature line and its narrative
    75	  When they are read
    76	  Then they name who the capability is for, what they can do, and the
    77	  outcome it serves, and that outcome is the framing's
    78	
    79	## Compile mapping (each Then → one judge-rubric assertion)
    80	
    81	| Scenario Then | Judge-rubric assertion |
    82	|---|---|
    83	| 1 — one observable behavior | "For each scenario and the Background: is the When one action, the Then observable in the running system, and no step implementation-specific? Cite any failing step." |
    84	| 2 — ownership and criteria | "For each scenario: is an owning shop named? Where a type is named, are both designer criteria present? Where constraints are said to be named, are they present? Cite or name the absence." |
    85	| 3 — identity tags | "For each scenario: `@feature:` present? `@hash:` present? Cite any scenario without both." |
    86	| 4 — edges covered | "For each row of the Edges table and each case the framing or a contributor's criteria name: a covering scenario or a reasoned exclusion? Any uncovered or missing case = fail." |
    87	| 5 — interaction types stated | "Does the Interaction types section name types, or 'none' with a reason the framing bears out? Cite the sentence or its absence." |
    88	| 6 — narrative | "Does the Feature narrative name who, what, and the outcome, and is the outcome the framing's? Cite the lines or their absence." |
