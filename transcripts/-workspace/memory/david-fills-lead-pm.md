Decided 2026-08-25: David (the product authority) fills `lead-pm` directly rather than sitting above an agent-PM. The role is agent-assisted: assist skills draft framing, run screens, and prepare decisions; David frames intent and runs the decisive checks. Budget: a daily review plus roadmap activities. `lead-po` is the maker (authors acceptance scenarios and backlog order within the framing); `lead-pm` is the checker. Grounded in `research:research/pm-po-one-role-2026-08.md` item 7.

**Why:** the only defensible ground for two roles is maker/checker (`define-good-up-front`); the execution-position split was a Claude Code artifact; David wants the system faster at outcomes, not more reviewed.

**How to apply:** route to David only decisions a definition can't answer or that are hard to reverse; every touch is a decision, not a read; asks carry a default; recurring asks become definition changes. Related: [[pm-dialogue-before-operationalizing]], [[safe-framework-stance]].
