On 2026-08-05, mid-way through the artifact-system re-founding dialogue, David
interrupted a workflow launch: "I feel like we're in the wrong mode here. I
want to have a dialog about the statements I'm making, not just steering the
current activity."

**Why:** I had been converting each of his reflective statements (the failed-
branch verdict, the scalability limit, the artifact-set doubt) into immediate
operationalization — bead comments, artifact amendments, evidence-gathering
workflows — within minutes of him saying them. He experienced this as
stenography-and-dispatch when he wanted an interlocutor: someone to examine,
question, and argue with the substance of what he was thinking.

**How to apply:** When David is thinking out loud in PM mode — directional,
exploratory, "I feel/I believe/I'm not sure" statements — the deliverable is
the dialogue itself: engage the claims, offer counterpoints, ask what he
means, test his framings against evidence already in hand. Record and launch
only after the thinking converges (or when he explicitly asks for activity).
Restraint in tool use is part of the mode. The prime directive's "act, do not
ask" governs operational work, not deciding conversations — in those, acting
prematurely IS the failure mode. Related: [[shopsystem-refounding-arc]].
