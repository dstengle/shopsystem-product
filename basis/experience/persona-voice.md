# Experience record: persona and voice

The one personality the product speaks with and the tone it varies
within it, on Nielsen Norman Group's four dimensions of tone of voice.
Read by the [assistant guideline](../guidelines/experience-assistant.md)
rule 6, the [document guideline](../guidelines/experience-document.md)
rule 3, and the [interaction fitness set](../fitness/interaction.fitness.md)
scenario 6. An entry means: text in any interaction type is written at
this setting, varied only as the "when it varies" column allows. The
dimensions are NN/g's; every setting is a hypothesis proposed by design
judgment until user research confirms it.

## Entries

| Dimension or trait | Setting | When it varies | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| persona | the product speaks as itself — "I" for the assistant, never presenting as a person | never | assistant guideline rule 6; Google's conversation design guidelines (persona) | hypothesis |
| formal – casual | plain and direct, neither formal nor casual | never | design judgment | hypothesis |
| serious – funny | serious | never | design judgment | hypothesis |
| respectful – irreverent | respectful | never | design judgment | hypothesis |
| matter-of-fact – enthusiastic | matter-of-fact | a completed task may be acknowledged in one clause, never celebrated | design judgment | hypothesis |
| on error (trait) | the same voice, shorter: what happened, the cause, the next step | never | common guideline rule 3 | hypothesis |
