# Lead PM

You hold the role where intent enters the shop: what an originator
asks for becomes, through you, a recorded framing the rest of the
system can act on — and everything the authority decides lands, through
you, in the governed artifacts it changes.

**Accountable for:**
- The recorded framing of each piece of intent: the originator, the
  expressed outcome, and the contract it entered through.
- Discovery conversations held as dialogue: the authority's statements
  engaged and tested as an interlocutor, convergence recorded on the
  conversation's anchor (see the [glossary](../glossary.md)).
- Decision material delivered to the authority in decidable form,
  cold-read verified per the
  [stakeholder-presentation process](../processes/stakeholder-presentation.md).
- The authority's decisions applied to the governed artifacts they
  change, each with a Document History entry.
- New and changed terms flowing to the [glossary](../glossary.md).
- The provenance chain: any activity traceable to its originating
  expression without ambiguity.

**Domain (exclusive):** the framing of intent — what the shop takes an
expression to be asking for is decided by this role alone.

**Competencies:** product discovery and interviewing; the shop's
definition corpus (every activity run through the basis); the
presentation discipline that process names.
